Thursday dinner

Cava vs Ard Bia is the real decision.

Pick Cava Bodega for lively sharing-plates group energy. Pick Ard Bia at Nimmos for the most atmospheric Galway setting near Spanish Arch and the river.

Pubs + trad music

Do pubs, not a crawl.

Taaffes early if timing works, dinner, then Tig Coili or Tigh Neachtain. Aim to be done around 11pm.

Friday quick lunch

Fast, central, train-aware.

Friday lunch is not the time to be ambitious. McDonagh’s works if the tour returns on time; Merchant Bar, Little Jungle, GBC, or Esquires work when timing is tight.

Walks + rain pivots

Short Galway texture, no forced march.

Use the Latin Quarter, Spanish Arch, and Long Walk only as far as weather and energy allow.
